Full-Time · Bangalore

Video Editor & Motion Designer

We're looking for a video editor who can turn raw footage into compelling content — reels, ad films, brand stories, and social videos that stop the scroll. You'll work closely with our creative and marketing teams, editing for multiple clients across industries. A strong eye for pacing, storytelling, colour, and platform-specific formats is essential.

Premiere Pro After Effects Reels & Shorts Colour Grading Brand Films Ad Creatives

What You'll Do

  • →Edit reels, short-form videos, and ad creatives for social media
  • →Produce brand films and promotional videos for clients
  • →Add motion graphics, transitions, captions, and sound design
  • →Format and optimise content for Instagram, YouTube, and Meta Ads
  • →Work with the team to ideate visual formats and storytelling angles

What We're Looking For

  • →Proficiency in Premiere Pro and/or After Effects
  • →Strong sense of pacing, storytelling, and visual composition
  • →Experience editing reels or short-form content (portfolio required)
  • →Ability to handle multiple projects and meet deadlines
  • →Bonus: motion graphics, colour grading, or CapCut experience
