Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you start working on my project?

Once we've completed the initial strategy session and scope agreement, most projects begin within 5–7 business days. Urgent timelines can be discussed on a case-by-case basis.

Do you work with businesses outside Bangalore?

Absolutely. We serve clients across India and work remotely with ease. Our processes are built for remote collaboration — regular video calls, shared dashboards, and structured reporting keep everything clear regardless of location.

What is the minimum engagement period?

For ongoing digital marketing retainers, we recommend a minimum of 3 months to see meaningful results from strategy and campaign optimisation. Project-based work (websites, videos, one-off campaigns) has no minimum engagement.

Do you offer packages or is everything custom?

Everything is custom. We don't believe in one-size-fits-all packages because no two businesses have the same goals, markets, or budgets. We build a scope that's right for you — and priced accordingly.

How do you report results and keep clients updated?

We provide monthly performance reports with clear metrics tied to your business goals — not vanity numbers. You'll also have access to live dashboards and a dedicated point of contact for all questions.

Can you handle both the marketing and the creative for the same campaign?

Yes — and this is one of our biggest advantages. Having both our Digital Marketing and Media Production teams under one roof means your ad creative, your strategy, and your campaigns are all aligned from day one with zero briefing gaps.
